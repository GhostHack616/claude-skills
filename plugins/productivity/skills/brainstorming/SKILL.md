# Brainstorming

Run a real brainstorm: diverge wide first, judge later, converge on what's actionable. Never deliver a single idea dressed as a brainstorm — quantity then selection is the method.

## Step 1 — Frame (30 seconds, not a questionnaire)

Restate the problem as a "How might we…?" question. If the goal or constraint is genuinely unknown, ask ONE question. Otherwise state your assumptions and go.

## Step 2 — Diverge (the core)

Generate **15-20 ideas minimum**, forced through different axes so they don't cluster:

- **Classique** — what a competent team would do
- **Inversé** — solve the opposite problem, or remove the assumed constraint
- **Emprunté** — how another industry solves this
- **Extrême budget zéro** — nothing to spend, only ingenuity
- **Extrême budget illimité** — what would 10x money unlock
- **Contrarian** — break the category convention
- **Combinatoire** — mash two existing assets/ideas of the user together

Rules: no judging during this phase, no feasibility filter, one line per idea, numbered. Wild ideas stay in — they seed good ones.

## Step 3 — Converge

Score every idea on **Impact (1-5) × Effort inversé (1-5)** in a compact table. Then pick:

- **Top 3** — high impact, realistic effort → each gets: why it wins, first concrete step, what could kill it
- **1 wildcard** — the idea you'd regret not mentioning, even if risky
- **Poubelle assumée** — name 2-3 discarded ideas and why (shows the filter worked)

## Step 4 — Hand off

End by asking which direction to push, and route the follow-up:
- idea needs deep challenge + execution blueprint → **gtm-action-thinker**
- plan needs stress-testing → **grill-me**
- needs copy/sequence/asset → the relevant copywriting or builder skill

## Output format

```
## How might we [problème reformulé] ?

### Divergence — N idées
1. … (Classique)
2. … (Inversé)
…

### Convergence
| # | Idée | Impact | Effort | Verdict |

### Top 3
1. **[Idée]** — pourquoi · premier pas · risque principal
…
🃏 Wildcard : …
🗑️ Écartées : …

➡️ On pousse laquelle ?
```
